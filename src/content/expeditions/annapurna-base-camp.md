## Objective

Reach Annapurna Base Camp at 4,130 meters. Understand what sustained physical effort under altitude and weather uncertainty teaches about decision-making.

## Preparation

Six months of cardiovascular training. Altitude acclimatization research. Gear selection driven by the same principle applied to software architecture: minimum viable weight, maximum functional coverage.

## Failure Points

Day 4: ignored early signs of altitude-related fatigue. Pushed pace to match the group instead of listening to my body. Paid for it on Day 5 with a forced rest day that nearly compromised the summit window.

Day 7: weather window collapsed. The decision to wait 18 hours instead of pushing through was the hardest of the trek. Every instinct said go. The mountain said wait.

## What the Mountain Taught

Preparation creates options. It does not eliminate uncertainty. The gap between a plan and reality is filled not by more planning, but by the quality of decisions made under pressure. Mountains do not reward ambition. They reward judgment.
