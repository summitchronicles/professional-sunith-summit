Summit Chronicles brings together three kinds of information that usually live apart: expedition writing, training data, and a record of progress over time.

## The problem

A journey across the Seven Summits produces more than a collection of trip reports. Training activity, recovery signals, photos, and field notes all need different ways to be captured and explored. A simple blog would leave much of that context disconnected.

## What I built

- A training dashboard that combines activity data from Intervals.icu with health metrics obtained through a Python integration with Garmin Connect.
- Semantic search for posts, expedition reports, and training data using Cohere embeddings and PostgreSQL with pgvector, with source links in responses.
- A content dashboard for drafting, previewing, and publishing Markdown-based stories, with image handling and automated build triggers.

The project is a place to test how data pipelines, content tools, and AI-assisted discovery can form one coherent product. It also documents the mountaineering journey that gave the platform its purpose.
